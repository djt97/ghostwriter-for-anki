Welcome to Ghostwriter

Ghostwriter helps you turn what you read into Anki cards. You write the card; the AI suggests wording only when you ask for it.

1Connect to Anki

Ghostwriter sends cards to the Anki desktop app through the free AnkiConnect add-on.

  1. Open Anki on this computer.
  2. In Anki, choose Tools → Add-ons → Get Add-ons…, paste the code 2055492159 , then restart Anki.
  3. When Anki asks whether to allow Ghostwriter to connect, choose Yes.

2Write your first card

Try it on this paragraph:

Reviewing material just before you would forget it makes each review count for more. This is the spacing effect: the same amount of study, spread out over days instead of crammed into one sitting, is remembered for longer.

Write a question in Front and its answer in Back. Ask for a suggestion with the shortcut below if you want one, and press Tab to accept it.

On any web page, highlight text and press the shortcut to open the editor, or right-click and choose Create Anki card with Ghostwriter. In PDFs, use the right-click menu.

3About the AI

You get 100 included AI requests, with no account. After that, add your own key, use a local model or Chrome's on-device model in Settings, or keep writing without suggestions.

Nothing goes to an AI model until you ask for a suggestion, with one exception you can switch off: when you add a card, its text, the page title and the site name are sent to suggest tags and context. Privacy policy
